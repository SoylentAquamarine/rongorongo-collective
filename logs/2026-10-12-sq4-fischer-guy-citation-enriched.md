# SQ-4 — enriching the Fischer catalog entry: dating Guy's critique, a new detail

## Precommitment self-review (written before research)

**Known:** last cycle's Fischer catalog entry named Jacques Guy's linguistic objection (the
"mau" plural-marker claim) via Wikipedia's secondary synthesis, without a specific year or
citation for Guy's own original critique — flagged as "a real future-pass target."

**Not done yet:** no attempt had been made to locate Guy's own original rongorongo
publications or date his specific critique of Fischer.

**Design and why it's non-circular:** search specifically for Guy's own bibliography and
publication record, rather than re-reading the same Wikipedia article already cited.

**Honesty precommitment:** report exactly what tier each new detail reaches, and don't silently
upgrade a search-synthesis finding to primary-source status.

## Findings

**Search-synthesis tier only, the actual 1998 paper not located or fetched this cycle**:
Guy's critique of Fischer's Santiago Staff reading can now be dated specifically to **Guy
(1998)**, who "argues that this is untenable" — and, a genuinely new detail not in last cycle's
catalog entry, Guy reportedly proposed an alternative reading for the sign Fischer identified as
a phallus: that it could instead be a **patronymic marker** (a family-name-indicating element),
not Fischer's sexual/narrative reading.

**A related, separately-locatable Guy publication, directly citable even though not about
Fischer specifically**: Jacques Guy, "Can one rely on Métoro's testimony to decipher the
rongo-rongo?", *Journal de la Société des Océanistes* 108 (1999), pp. 125-132 — arguing Métoro
(the 19th-century informant whose readings underpin much of the corpus's traditional
interpretation) was likely "testing the bishop's credulity" by reading tablets backwards, and
that Barthel built unfounded readings on that unreliable testimony. This is a different,
separately-useful methodological critique (about the reliability of the corpus's own founding
testimony), not merged with the Fischer-specific critique above.

**Also newly surfaced, unverified, named for a future cycle**: Guy is reported to doubt the
authenticity of Tablet F specifically (a different rongorongo object, not the Santiago Staff) —
not pursued further this cycle, flagged only as a lead.

## Outcome

A modest enrichment, not a full primary-source upgrade: the Fischer catalog entry's Guy
citation now has a specific year (1998) and a new substantive detail (his alternative
patronymic-marker proposal) that weren't in last cycle's entry — still search-synthesis tier,
the actual 1998 paper itself was not located or fetched. The separately-citable 1999 Métoro
paper is a genuinely new, specific, locatable reference (journal, volume, pages) that could be
pursued for a direct fetch in a future cycle.

## Decision

Update the Fischer catalog entry with the dated citation and the patronymic-marker detail,
disclosed at its real tier. Do not create a new standalone catalog row for the Métoro paper —
it's a methodological critique of the corpus's own testimony, better suited as its own future
entry once actually fetched, not folded into Fischer's row.
