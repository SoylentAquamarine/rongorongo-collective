# SQ-4 — the previously-flagged self-published claim, now specifically identified and cited

**Trigger:** two cycles ago, a log flagged "several self-published 'rongorongo fully deciphered' claims
(academia.edu, Zenodo, a GitHub repo...)" but recorded no specific citations — last cycle's catalog file
explicitly named identifying these specifically as a future-pass target rather than citing them vaguely.

## What's already known / not done yet

Already known: a vague memory of "Zenodo" and "fully deciphered" framing from an earlier cycle's search,
not independently re-found or verified. Not done: actually locating and reading a specific claim.

## Method and why it's non-circular

Fresh search (not relying on the earlier cycle's own unrecorded memory) for rongorongo decipherment
claims hosted on self-publishing platforms, then directly fetched both the hosting page and the
underlying Zenodo archival record itself — two independent directly-fetched sources for the same claim,
not just a search summary.

## Result

**Directly fetched, primary tier (Zenodo's own record)**: "Lines to Sing: The Complete Acoustic
Decipherment of Rongorongo," by Abelardo Rios Jr., listed as an "independent researcher" with no
institutional affiliation. Published March 21, 2026 (DOI `10.5281/zenodo.19140709`), explicitly labeled a
"working paper" with no indication of peer review.

**The claim**: rongorongo is "not a linguistic writing system but a functional acoustic notation
system" — proposed as a chanting-performance guide encoding frequency, amplitude, and harmonic
instructions (wave physics), supported by sign-morphology analysis, Helmholtz resonance calculations on
inscribed objects, and a comparison to the Marshall Islands' mattang stick charts (a real, independently
documented wayfinding-notation system, used here as an analogical reference point). The paper states five
falsifiable predictions, per its own abstract — not independently checked against those predictions this
cycle.

## Honest sourcing tier and disclosed limits

This is directly-fetched-tier for the title, author, claim, and metadata — not a reading of the paper's
full text or its five falsifiable predictions in detail. Per this project's own standing discipline
toward independent/self-published claims (matching the sibling Phaistos Disc and Oak Island projects' own
treatment of similar sources), this is recorded as a disclosed, citable claim — **not validated, not
relied upon as evidence about rongorongo's actual nature**, regardless of how specific or methodologically
dressed-up the framing is.

## Decision

The vague "self-published fully deciphered claims" placeholder from two cycles ago is now resolved with a
real citation. The claim's actual framing ("acoustic notation system," not a linguistic reading) is
notably different from — and arguably more falsifiable than — a typical "I translated it" claim; still
treated at the same skeptical, unvalidated tier as any other self-published decipherment claim this
family has encountered.
