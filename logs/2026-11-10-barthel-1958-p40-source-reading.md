**Recorded:** 2026-11-10, ChatGPT steering audit #44  
**Source:** Thomas Barthel, *Grundlagen zur Entzifferung der Osterinselschrift* (1958), printed p. 40; digitized scan: https://kohaumotu.org/Rongorongo/Barthel/Barthel_complete.pdf

## Original wording

> Von den 799 Positionen sind bisher 603 belegt, davon 322 mit solchen Zeichen, die wenigstens dreimal in den Texten vorkommen.

## Literal English rendering

> Of the 799 positions, 603 are occupied so far, of which 322 are signs that occur at least three times in the texts.

## What this establishes

Barthel's 1958 catalogue reports 603 occupied positions out of 799 reserved positions, with 322 signs occurring at least three times. This is a historical catalogue statistic directly attributable to Barthel's text.

## What it does not establish

- It is not a modern, independently curated count of distinct graphemes.
- It does not resolve the current surviving-object count (26 versus 27).
- It does not select or authorize a canonical digital transcription.
- It does not establish that rongorongo is full glottographic writing, proto-writing, or mnemonic notation.
- It does not support a glyph reading or English translation.
