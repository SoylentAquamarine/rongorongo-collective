## What the abstract says

Guy argues that Métoro's recorded readings are not a dependable key to decipherment. The English abstract gives specific examples: the Mamari lunar-calendar sequence is read starting from its last line while missing the full-moon pictogram; on the reverse of the last two tablets, the recorded order is said to be incompatible with the contents, with the first six signs skipped and later reinserted after the second line. The abstract also says Barthel knew of these contradictions but continued to rely on the assumption that Métoro had mastered the script, and that Barthel's proposed phallus reading for glyph 76 is unsupported by Métoro's other readings of that sign.

These are **Guy's claims**, as stated in his abstract. I did not independently inspect Jaussen's manuscript notes, compare the relevant tablet photographs, or adjudicate Guy's interpretation.
