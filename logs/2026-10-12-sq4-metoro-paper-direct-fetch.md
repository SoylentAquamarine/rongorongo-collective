## Findings

**Located**: `persee.fr/doc/jso_0300-953x_1999_num_108_1_2083` (an initially-guessed ID,
`...2062`, was wrong and returned HTTP 404 — corrected via Persée's own search, not silently
retried until something worked).

**Directly fetched, new specific detail not previously on file**: the paper's background
section names Métoro's own claimed teaching lineage — "Métoro Tauara... prétendant avoir
étudié jadis cette écriture sous la direction" of three named lettrés: **Ngahu, Rei-miro, and
Paovaa**. It also specifies exactly which four tablets Bishop Jaussen showed Métoro and
recorded readings for: **Aruku-Kurenga, Tahua, Mamari, and Keiti** — with Barthel (1958) later
reproducing Jaussen's notes, hieroglyphic texts included in an appendix.

**Not reached by this fetch, disclosed rather than silently filled in from the earlier
search-synthesis summary**: the actual argument for why Guy considers Métoro's testimony
unreliable (the "testing the bishop's credulity by reading tablets in increasing disorder"
claim, and the specific fault found with Barthel's handling) is **not** present in the fetched
page content — it's in a section (likely the paper's own conclusion, behind a further link or
page) this fetch didn't reach. The search-synthesis summary from last cycle describing that
argument remains at its original tier, not upgraded by this fetch.

## Outcome

A real, partial upgrade: the paper's setup/background is now directly verified with new,
specific detail (the three teachers, the four tablets) that search-synthesis alone hadn't
surfaced. The paper's actual central argument remains unverified at primary-source tier — a
