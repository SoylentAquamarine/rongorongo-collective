## Reproduction limits and scope
Direct page opens in this environment failed (copyright page HTTP 502; legacy digitized corpus page timeout), although a fresh search returned the official copyright page's full indexed wording. This is stronger than a third-party assertion, but it is not a formal Creative Commons licence.

The explicit statement applies to contents of the legacy CEIPP site, subject to non-profit distribution and source acknowledgment. It does not automatically settle rights for the newer corpus site, third-party materials, transformed datasets, or any use outside those conditions. No corpus bytes were downloaded, copied, normalized, or redistributed.

## Decision

The Director should compare the legacy transliteration against the newer corpus site, record exactly which content is covered, and decide whether the stated conditions satisfy this project's planned public noncommercial research use. Obtain written clarification for any uncertain derivative/redistribution use.
