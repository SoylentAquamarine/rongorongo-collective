widely-cited 1990 lunar-calendar reading derives from Rjabchikov's own earlier (1989) work, not an
independent discovery.

This is a directly-fetched primary read of Rjabchikov's own paper (not a search snippet) — but it reports

counter-source (e.g. a response from Guy, or a third party's assessment of the priority question) was
