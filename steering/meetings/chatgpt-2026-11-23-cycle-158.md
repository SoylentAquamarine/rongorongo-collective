## Evidence standard and falsification

A writing-system hypothesis must predict unseen sign sequences and positional constraints across a defined corpus, with a frozen sign inventory and explicit alternatives. Reject a proposed reading if it depends on arbitrary sign values, selective examples, unsupported segmentation, or fails held-out sequences. A mnemonic hypothesis must likewise make testable predictions rather than serve as an unfalsifiable fallback.

## Ethics and corpus permissions
